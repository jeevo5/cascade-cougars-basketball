PROGRAM ANNOUNCEMENT

WORKOUTS / OPEN GYM START SEPT. 30

Cascade Boys Basketball workouts and open gyms begin Wednesday, September 30 and continue every Monday and Wednesday from 7:00–8:30 PM. Regular location is the Cougar Den. October 5 and October 7 will be held in the Old Gym.
